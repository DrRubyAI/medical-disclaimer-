We do not
  • Diagnose any medical condition
  • Prescribe or recommend medications

We do

Section 04

How we communicate findings

We are deliberate about the language we use. DrRuby observes and tracks — we do not diagnose or prescribe. Here are examples of how this shows up in practice:

We don't say"You have inflammation."
We say"Your Inflammation Index has increased 12% over 4 weeks. We recommend discussing this with your dermatologist."
We don't say"This supplement will fix your collagen."
We say"Your Collagen Proxy shows a positive trend since adding this supplement. Consult your physician before making changes."
We don't say"You are in perimenopause."
We say"Your patterns are consistent with hormonal transition. We strongly recommend speaking with your OB/GYN or a women's health specialist."
Section 05

We always recommend consulting a doctor

DrRuby is designed to complement — not replace — your relationship with your healthcare providers. We actively encourage every user to:

  • Maintain a regular relationship with a primary care physician
  • Consult a board-certified dermatologist for skin concerns
  • Work with a women's health specialist for hormonal and perimenopause-related questions
  • Bring DrRuby data and trends to their clinical appointments as a conversation starter

If any insight from DrRuby raises concern, please contact a qualified healthcare professional promptly. Do not delay seeking medical care because of anything you have read or observed on this platform.
